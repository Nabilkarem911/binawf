## Methodology

Pages were fetched via HTTP and parsed for image counts, YouTube embeds, Google Drive links, iframes, and text content. Every page on the site shares an identical navigation menu (~2800 chars of text, 1 header banner image, ~148 nav links). The counts below reflect **total images on the page including the shared header**. As a rule of thumb:
- **1–3 images** = placeholder/index page (header only, no real content)
- **4–20 images** = moderate content (gallery or article with photos)
- **20+ images** = rich content gallery
- **150+ images** = very large gallery

All images are hosted on `lh3.googleusercontent.com/sitesv/` (Google Sites internal CDN).

## Image Hosting

All images are hosted on Google's CDN: `https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/sitesv/AG8ngQ...`

Image URLs follow the pattern:
```
https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/sitesv/{base64-encoded-id}=w{width}
```

The first image on every page (`AG8ngQXL7w5...`) is the shared site header/banner — not page content. Content images have different IDs and typically use `=w1280` or `=w16383` size parameters.
